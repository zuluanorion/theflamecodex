Dr. Zuluan Orion

Originator of Quantum Transformation · Cognizer of the Light Codes

I was born in Bogotá in 1971. I grew up in a hard home, and the emptiness it left in my heart became the beginning of my spiritual path. I needed to find something, though I didn't know what.

That search took me through more than sixty thousand hours of meditation and almost two decades living and studying in Asia: twelve and a half years in India, with yoga, meditation and Ayurvedic medicine, and eight in Thailand, with Buddhism and healing. Along the way an awakening to the Self came, and in nearly thirty years it has not reversed.

Out of that life came the two things I offer. Quantum Transformation is the system I created to guide the soul through its own levels of Consciousness, to the root of what hurts, and back to its light. The Light Codes are a sacred language of syllables and sigils that I received and recognized, and they live at the heart of the Flame Codex.

For two decades I have brought them together in my clinic in Weston, Florida, with Ayurvedic, natural and quantum medicine, and with people from many countries.

I am not the source. Consciousness is. My work is to return you to the light you never lost.

CO-DIRECTOR OF SPIRITUAL BALANCE

Grace Orion

Psychology (FIU) · Quantum Transformation · Reiki Master · Speaker and author

I am Grace Orion, a Psychology graduate of Florida International University (FIU), specialized in Quantum Transformation, a Reiki Master, speaker and author. My calling is to accompany people as they come to understand their inner world, recognize the patterns that shape their lives, and develop a more conscious relationship with themselves.

In my sessions I work with the emotions, beliefs and experiences that can influence how a person sees themselves, relates to others and meets their challenges. I accompany processes of self-knowledge, stress management, strengthening self-esteem and transforming emotional patterns, as well as moments of change that call for clarity, guidance and a space to be heard.

Quantum Transformation is the axis of my practice. From this approach I integrate the emotional, physical, energetic and spiritual dimensions as interrelated aspects of human experience. My purpose is for each person to understand what they are living through, recognize their inner resources, and carry that understanding into concrete changes in their daily life.

My training in psychology is complemented by more than 30 years of experience in Reiki and meditation, as well as training in biofeedback and bioresonance. These tools are brought in according to the needs and goals of each process, within close and personalized accompaniment.

As co-director of Spiritual Balance, I work alongside Dr. Zuluan Orion in an integral approach to well-being. My work focuses especially on emotional accompaniment and the development of personal awareness, offering a space to explore what needs attention and to cultivate greater balance, autonomy and meaning in life.
