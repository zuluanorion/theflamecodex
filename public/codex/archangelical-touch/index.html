THE SACRED, IN YOUR HANDS

There are moments when we need to find our center again

To feel accompanied. To find clarity. To make room in the heart, or to gather the courage for a step we have been putting off.

Archangelical Touch was born as an invitation to take part consciously in that process. It is a system of energetic and spiritual practice that I have developed, bringing together the tracing of symbols, intention and the sensitivity of the hands, to work with what this teaching recognizes as archangelic energy.

Its purpose is to bring the sacred close to your life: to how you feel, how you relate, how you care for your spaces, and how you give form to what you wish to build.

A SYMBOL YOU TRACE

An intention that comes to life

Imagine stopping for a few moments, feeling your breath, and recognizing what you need to cultivate right now: calm, stability, clarity, courage.

With a conscious movement of your hand, you trace a sigil: a symbol with a specific form and sequence. Your attention follows the gesture. Your intention finds a direction. You allow yourself to be present and receptive.

In Archangelical Touch, these symbols are used as keys of connection with different qualities of archangelic energy. The learning is to recognize them, receive them, and direct their expression through your hands and your attention.

The symbol stops being only an image you contemplate. It becomes a gesture you make, an experience you explore, and a quality you seek to embody.

YOUR HANDS

An expression of presence and care

If you know Reiki, you will find a point of reference: Archangelical Touch is also an energetic practice that uses the hands and intention. It also has its own language of sigils, its own working keys, and a particular relationship with the archangelic currents and the Light Codes.

In this system, to polarize energy means to direct it toward a defined quality and purpose. You can work with it in three ways:

Your own fieldwhat this teaching calls the aura
Another personsomeone who wishes to receive the practice
Objects and spacesan act of blessing and conscious care

The heart of it is learning to do this with sensitivity: knowing when to open the practice, how to hold the intention, and when to close, let go and return to everyday life.

WHAT IT CAN BRING TO YOUR LIFE

Five gifts of the practice

YOUR EMOTIONAL HEALING

A space to accompany your emotional healing

When you have been holding too much for too long, stopping with attention and tenderness can mean a great deal. The practice offers a spiritual space to listen to what you feel, to allow yourself to rest, and to grow a kinder relationship with yourself.

YOUR INTENTIONS

Rootedness to give form to your intentions

Perhaps you have an idea, a project or a change you want to make. You can bring that intention into the practice and accompany it with concrete decisions. Bringing something into being also begins with finding clarity, getting organized and holding the next step.

YOUR ENERGY FIELD

A conscious way to care for your energy field

Working with the aura is a practice of harmony and presence: a chance to stop, to recognize how you are living, and to grow the qualities you want to express more deeply.

THE PEOPLE YOU LOVE

A way to share care with others

With their consent, you can offer a space of attention and spiritual accompaniment to someone close to you. Learning to offer also means listening, respecting their limits and leaving them completely free in their experience.

YOUR OBJECTS AND SPACES

A sacred meaning in your objects and spaces

A journal, a piece of jewellery or a meditation object can become a reminder of an intention. Your home or your place of practice can receive a gesture of blessing that goes along with the real care you give them.

THE LIGHT CODES BECOME A LIVING PRACTICE

From silence to your hands, and from your hands to your life

The Flame Codex brings its syllables, its sigils and its contemplative qualities. Archangelical Touch carries that language into the gesture of the hands and into the art of sharing from presence.

As I see it, this is a movement of incarnation: that what you recognize in silence finds expression in your body, in your relationships and in your actions.

Calm can become a different way of responding.
Courage, a conversation long put off.
Clarity, a decision.
Intention, something you begin to build.

That is where the practice comes alive: when what you cultivate inside begins to transform the way you live.

FLAME BEARERS AND FLAME KEEPERS

Who can accompany you

You can begin on your own, with a book and a quiet corner. But walking with someone who already knows the way makes everything simpler and deeper. In the Codex we speak of two kinds of companions:

FLAME BEARERS

Bearers of the Flame

People in whom the Flame is already incarnating. They live the process of the Descent in their own body and life, and accompany you from that lived experience. No certificate makes a Bearer: the Descent does.

FLAME KEEPERS

Keepers of the Flame

People who have prepared themselves and are attuning, so that this same process awakens in them. They care for the language of the Codex with faithfulness, ethics and love.
