THE 21 KEYS OF ARCHANGELICAL TOUCH

A path of presence, connection and inner transformation

There are moments when we need to stop, return to our center, and listen to what life is asking us to transform.

Archangelical Touch brings together 21 keys across three levels. Each one offers a focus for spiritual practice: grounding yourself, relating to your emotions, strengthening your will, expressing what you carry inside, and deepening clarity, discernment and service.

Within this teaching, the keys are understood as symbols that guide archangelic connection and channeling. The practice brings together geometry, intention and presence, accompanied by a specific initiation and training.

The purpose is for spiritual experience to find a place in your life: in how you care for yourself, how you relate, what decisions you make, and what you are willing to build.

Level I · ElementalLevel II · ConsciousnessLevel III · Master Cosmic

INITIATION REQUIRED

The keys of Archangelical Touch require initiation.

According to the teaching of this system, for the keys to work as tools of this practice, a person must have been properly initiated by a Keeper of the Flame (Flame Keeper) authorized to give that initiation, or by a Bearer of the Flame (Flame Bearer).

Seeing the symbol, downloading its image, copying it or saying its name does not replace initiation.

Initiation brings a person into the method. The training teaches how to use it, how to direct intention, and the scope and responsibilities of the practice.

Receiving an initiation does not guarantee a particular result, nor does it automatically authorize you to initiate or teach others.

Learn about the initiation process

HOW THE PRACTICE WORKS

Four elements

Working with the keys brings together four elements:

1InitiationReceiving the corresponding initiation from someone authorized to transmit it.
2IntentionRecognizing clearly which quality you wish to cultivate and what the purpose of the practice is.
3Tracing and presenceWorking with the original symbol and conscious attention, following the guidance received during the training.
4IntegrationGiving the experience continuity through decisions, habits and actions.

The procedures for activation, pronunciation, tracing and application are taught in the training; they should not be deduced or improvised from the images on this page.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

A space to gather yourself, observe your experience, connect with your spirituality and work with an intention.

The experience is personal. It may feel like calm, reflection, emotion or a new perspective; it can also be subtle, without any special sensations. Feeling more intensity does not show greater effectiveness.

Transformation is cultivated with continuity. Bringing an intention into form also means choosing, acting, learning and sustaining what you want to develop.

FLAME BEARERS AND FLAME KEEPERS

Who can accompany you

You can begin on your own, with a book and a quiet corner. But walking with someone who already knows the way makes everything simpler and deeper. In the Codex we speak of two kinds of companions:

FLAME BEARERS

Bearers of the Flame

People in whom the Flame is already incarnating. They live the process of the Descent in their own body and life, and accompany you from that lived experience. No certificate makes a Bearer: the Descent does.

FLAME KEEPERS

Keepers of the Flame

People who have prepared themselves and are attuning, so that this same process awakens in them. They care for the language of the Codex with faithfulness, ethics and love.
