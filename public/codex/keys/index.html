THE WHOLE SYSTEM

One hundred and seventy-seven doors into yourself

Think of white light passing through a prism. The light is still one, yet it reveals itself in many colours. These keys are like that: each one expresses a quality of one same Flame, the one that already lives in you.

Each key has a name you can say, a plate you can contemplate, a sign that can be traced, and a meaning you can carry into your day. Some hold. Others release. Some bring order, protect, open, seal, reveal, remember, root, harmonize or transmute.

You do not need to know them all. You can work deeply with a single one and let it keep you company for years. Depth never depends on how many: it depends on your relationship with it.

WHAT THEY CAN OPEN IN YOUR LIFE

To clear what covers your Flame

The keys do not give you something you lack. They help you remove what darkens your light: ways of thinking that keep repeating, emotions held for years, memories the body has carried for a long time, patterns from your family and your culture that you never chose. When that begins to let go, life starts to move differently.

HOW TO LIVE WITH A KEY

Drink. Use the Codex.

A person can study the properties of water for years and die of thirst without having drunk a single drop. That is why I invite you to live them:

  • Carry a syllable with you during the day.
  • Say it before you make a decision.
  • Contemplate its plate in silence.
  • Place it in your awareness before you sleep, and notice which dreams appear.
  • Use a triad to accompany a transition.
  • And then, live.
Presence is worth more than repetition.

You can say a syllable just once, completely present, and then let the silence come. Listen to it. Feel what happens. Notice your body, your breath, your emotions. And notice too if nothing happens at all: there are deep processes that are silent.
