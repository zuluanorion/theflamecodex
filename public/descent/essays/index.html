THE TWO BOOKS, PART BY PART

Read the Descent slowly

The Yoga of the Descent was born as two books. The first, the Door Book, accompanies the moment when something begins to happen in an ordinary life. The second, the Book of the Dwelling, shows the architecture that holds it.

Here I have gathered them in 42 essays, one for each Part, so that you can walk through them at your own pace. Each one ends with a short practice taken from the book itself.

You do not need to read them in order. You can begin where your life is today: with the door, with the body, with the heart, or with the word.

The second book
