The Descent

The Yoga of the Descent · Branch I · The Door

The Descent · the essaysI · The Door03 / 42
“You do not make the light. You do not push it. You make room.”From Yoga del Descenso · Libro Puerta

Perhaps something is happening to you that you did not expect. Your inner life is beginning to mature in earnest and, instead of feeling more elevated, you feel simpler. Instead of visions, you have presence while peeling fruit. Instead of ecstasy, you feel a growing tenderness for your ordinary life. And a very widespread spiritual picture, the one that says growing is above all climbing and letting go of what is here, whispers to you that you are failing.

If this is happening to you, listen to me carefully: you are not going down a level. You are becoming a dwelling.

Here we reach the center of the book, and precisely for that reason it is where I owe you the most honesty. We do not know how to prove what Presence is in itself. I cannot prove it to you, and I am a little wary of anyone who claims to know it with absolute certainty. Perhaps you do know it, in your own way, by faith, and that knowing of yours does not need my permission. What I can do with you is more modest and more useful: describe how its arrival is usually lived and what changes seem to come with it. Your faith will speak of the what.

The way dawn arrives

Almost all of us carry inside an image of how something spiritual ought to arrive: as a current that enters through the crown of the head, the chest or the forehead and runs through the body the way water runs through a pipe. Many accounts do not fit that image well. I would rather offer you another: the dawn.

Morning light does not enter a house through a pipe. It arrives over the house, and each room receives it according to what that room is. The room with open windows fills at once. The room with heavy curtains barely notices that day has come. The light is the same; each room’s response is different. The Presence does not have to travel through your body for your body to respond to it. This is an image and not a proven mechanism, but it is the one that best respects what people tell: the warmth here, the pressure there and the stillness in the chest would be the rooms of your house responding, each in its own way and at its own speed.

It seems a small difference, and it changes everything. If the Presence were a current, your work would be to clear a path for it, to push it, to direct it, and from there come years of exhausting effort. If it arrives the way dawn does, your work is much simpler and much deeper: to open curtains.

And it does not always arrive the same way. Sometimes as peace, a sweet weight, a stillness with a body. Sometimes as clarity, as if someone had cleaned a pane of glass you had been looking through for years. Sometimes as serene strength, or as a quiet joy that celebrates nothing and needs nothing. And sometimes without a face, only as a silent certainty that you are not alone. None of these faces is higher than another. They are ways of arriving, not grades.

Downward

Notice where all of this points. Peace does not take you out of the kitchen: it settles into the kitchen. Clarity does not carry you to another world: it shows you this one. Serene strength does not lift you above your life: it plants you more firmly inside it.

I call the Descent the movement by which what you live spiritually begins to enter ever more deeply into your concrete life. Not every spiritual movement is the Descent, and this one is not a decline of the experience. I invite you to read it as another way of maturing: what you found above, learning to live below.

When this happens, different parts of you may respond, in no required order. The mind, which slows down or stops. The emotions, which soften or thaw. The breath, which deepens on its own and seems bilingual, because it speaks the language of the soul and of the body at the same time. The body, with its warmth, its coolness, its weeping and its deep stillness. And, more slowly than all the rest, habits and identity. But none of this turns every slow thought or every warmth into proof that day has broken: the body has a thousand reasons to do these things, and some of them are attended to in a doctor’s office. And if your process begins somewhere else, nothing is wrong. Your experience rules over the map I offer you. Always.

The owner of the house

It remains to say who that luminous guest is, the one who comes to the house and seems to withdraw.

No experiment or argument establishes it, and no one can demand that you believe it on their authority. But there is a way of knowing that is not argued: it is remembered. When it happens, no new information arrives. Something recognizes itself. From that reading, which is the one I hold and which cannot be proven, the guest does not come from outside.

It is not a visit. It is the owner of the house learning to inhabit it.

I call that light the Flame, and not because of heat: I call it that because it burns without consuming anything and shines without explaining itself. It is prior to form, prior to name, prior to any concept, including mine. What descends is that light becoming form: not a foreign force occupying a body that does not belong to it, but what you already were, recognizing form as its own dwelling. That is why the warmth in the chest or the breath that deepens on its own is not the noise the flesh makes while the important thing happens. It is the important thing, happening as flesh.

Imagine that this light, in order to see itself, becomes countless living mirrors, each with its own history, its own will and its own name. You are one of them, and the surface through which you reflect is called the mind. Over the years dust settles: fear, defenses, the idea of who you are, the habit of needing every thought as proof that you exist. None of that is malice. It is dust, and when the mirror hardly gives anything back, we call that normal life.

The light never changed. It did not go out, it did not withdraw, it did not increase when you saw it again.

The only thing that changes is what can reflect it. That is why you can care for the house but you cannot manufacture the dawn, which never went away. Recognition does not light the light: it cleans what kept you from seeing it. But for the light to stay and live there, recognition is needed, because no one fully inhabits a house where they are not recognized as belonging to it.

A door that opens

And why is it so hard? At some point the mind took charge. We handed it the government of the physical, of time and of space, and that was neither a mistake nor a betrayal: it was a tool doing the only thing it knows how to do, which is to avoid harm. To manage that, it put up a door. But a closed door does not distinguish directions. It keeps out what could hurt you and, with the same gesture, keeps in what you are. Hence the feeling of being separate, of there being an inside and an outside and of being alone on the inside.

The mind has no being of its own. It is not an enemy to be defeated, because it is not someone. That is why I will not ask you to destroy it. It is not dethroned as a punishment. It is dethroned because the throne belonged to another. The Descent does not tear down that door. It opens it. And the mind is not dismissed from the house: it no longer has to guard the entrance, because what was outside turned out to live within.

The eternal becomes human

Yoga means union, and almost all the paths that bear that name seek that union upward. They do well, because for centuries seeking above was the way of finding. This book describes the opposite movement. The first ascent sought to find. The Descent learned to embody. And the second ascent does not escape: it expresses what it has already learned to live below.

The book says it in the voice of what is neither born nor dies: “I allow myself to be entirely finite. I allow myself to have an hour, hunger, tiredness and a biography.” And at no moment is it in exile, because it did not come to escape this house: it came to inhabit it.

And one safeguard: this does not make you infallible. The nature of what descends is not a permission. You still need to discern, you can still be wrong, you still owe consent, limits and care to the people around you, and you still need doctors when the body asks for them and company when the mind asks for it. A cleaner mirror is not a superior mirror, not least because the light it reflects is not its own.

If someone asked you what kind of love this is, the answer fits in a single line: the eternal becomes human so that it can be lived.

FOR YOUR PRACTICE

When something moves in you, do not push it or chase it: no one produces the dawn; you open the window.

Notice what responds in your house, the mind, the emotions, the breath, the body or your habits, without turning it into proof of anything.

Describe first, understand later. Your experience rules over the map.
