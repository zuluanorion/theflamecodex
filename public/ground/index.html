The Ground

The nondual awakening of Consciousness

Before any code, any sigil or any practice, there is one recognition. Everything else on this site rests on it.

For years I lived as if the mind were the centre of my life. The mind searched, argued, suffered, desired, feared, interpreted, defended and named. And then attention turned toward the one who was aware of the mind.

Something loosened. The mind was seen as movement. The witness was seen as stillness. The personal self was seen as a wave within a greater ocean. The wave had never been separate from the ocean; it had only been fascinated by its own form.

What is this life? What is suffering?
Who is the one who suffers? Who is the one who looks?
And what remains when the one who looks is finally seen?

Not a belief

This recognition is not a doctrine to accept or a philosophy to defend. It is direct. It is what the great nondual teachers — Ramana Maharshi, Papaji — point to with a single question: turn inward and discover who or what you truly are. Not as belief. Not as spiritual clothing. As recognition.

When it happened for me it was simple, almost comic. I laughed: «It can't be that easy.» In the days that followed the personality was seen for what it is: an event within Consciousness. That recognition has not reversed in nearly thirty years.

Why it comes first

The Flame Codex is built on this ground for one reason: the source of liberation is not the facilitator, not the code and not the method. The source is Consciousness itself. A wound does not heal because someone hands the person something. It frees itself because the person's own Consciousness recognizes what was false and returns to what was always true.

I describe my own realization as the recognition of the nonlocal essence of Consciousness through a finite form. Not the descent of a god, not the ascent of a superhuman. A finite human life in which the Infinite recognizes itself.

But recognition is not the whole movement. Knowing you are the ocean is not the same as every drop of the body being saturated by it.
