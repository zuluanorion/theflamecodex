THE PHILOSOPHY BENEATH THE CODEX

One recognition

Before any code, any sigil or any practice, there is one recognition. Everything else on this site rests on it.

Your thoughts and your feelings come and go, and your body changes with time. But there is always a part of your Being that never changes and remains the same: the silent observer that is always there.

Separation is only a feeling, not a reality. The one who notices that feeling is the one who is revealed.

Here I gather what I have written in my books about the awakening of the Self, nondual reality and devotion: 32 essays to read slowly, to observe yourself, and to come back home.

The awakening of the SelfRecognizing, in the middle of your own life, what you have always been. It all begins with one question, asked from the heart: who am I?
Nondual realityEven if you see yourself in a body separate from others, in essence we are one and the same. What you do to another, you do to yourself.
DevotionWe are part of the Divinity and we hold a relationship with it. This is what I have called monodualism: moving from religion to relationship.

FROM AWAKENING TO THE CODEX

The philosophy and the practice

The Flame Codex is the practical teaching; this philosophy is what holds it up.

The Light Codes are not symbols chosen at random. They are expressions of this divine Consciousness, and they are there to help us attune our psyche and our expression to our true nature, bringing clarity and the descent of divine, truer aspects of ourselves into the experience of the human psyche.

IIlluminationThe recognition of the Being: the root everything rests on.
IIThe DescentWhat was recognized above must come down, into the body and into ordinary life.IIIThe Flame CodexThe language born inside that descent: the Light Codes, the practical teaching.

THE TREE

Seven branches, one path home

Read them in order, or begin wherever your heart asks. Each essay ends with a short practice taken from my books.

BRANCH I

The call

When life begins to ask

It all begins with a restlessness: a happiness we cannot find outside, a pain that asks to be looked at, a question that will not let us go. The wish to awaken comes from our true Being.

01The source of your own happinessHappiness is not chased: it is allowed.02Where suffering is bornThe deepest suffering is not knowing who you really are; facing it without fear breaks the shell.03Who am I? The question the soul carriesThe question the soul carries so as not to get lost: the mind asks, the heart answers.

BRANCH II

What you are not

Body, mind and emotions

Your thoughts and your feelings come and go, and none of them can contain your essence. Your body and your thoughts are energy moving in Consciousness. When you see clearly what you are not, you see who the witness is.

04Your body and your mind are not who you areSeeing first what you are not, to discover what remains.05The mind has no being of its ownThe “I” is a handful of thoughts: the mind cannot live without you, but you exist without it.06Emotions: energy moving in youEmotions are energy in motion; you are what notices them.07The body within ConsciousnessYou are not trapped in the body: the body moves within Consciousness.

BRANCH III

The one who observes

The witness and the I Am

There is a silent observer that is always there, a consciousness that never changes and that nothing can alter. That relationship with consciousness is the great door to the I Am, which can never be closed.

08The feeling of lifeConsciousness is not produced by the brain: it is the simple feeling of being alive.09What has never changed in youThere is a part of your Being that has never changed and remains the same.10Who is the one looking?A witness that is never disturbed: the Observer is what you really are.11The mystery of the I AmThe I Am in you is the I Am of God, and it is recognized in silence.

BRANCH IV

The prisons of the mind

How the person is formed

Domestication, added to cellular memory and karma, forms what we call the person. But none of it reaches the soul, or the Being, much less consciousness.

12Domestication + cellular memory + karma = personWhat we learned, what we inherited and karma form the person; what is essential in you was never domesticated.13Karma and dharma: action without frictionWhat you think you do to another, you do to yourself; dharma is serving beyond the ego.14The ego: the feeling of separationThe ego, the feeling of separation, is born in time and dies in time.15The prison of the mindInherited beliefs, a prison you cannot see, and a question that opens it: “is it so?”16The seeker: the ego's last maskEven spirituality can become one more search: the step is to stop seeking.

BRANCH V

The present

The practice of being here

To be present, above all, is to be in the truth. There is nothing spiritual in the mind.

17Now, now, nowLive in the present: everything is transformed in the now, in ordinary days too.18To meditate is to observeTo meditate is to become aware of what happens within you: the mind settles like a lake.19Breathe it: staying with painPain that is looked at and breathed with presence lets go.

BRANCH VI

The heart

Devotion, love and monodualism

Although you are one with everything, God is God and is always there. We are part of the Divinity and we hold a relationship with it: this is what I have called monodualism. Move from religion to relationship.

20From religion to relationshipHow you treat life is how you treat God.21Monodualism: one with everything, and God is GodWe are part of the Divinity, but we are not the Divinity: oneness without devotion is incomplete.22Innocence and divine ignoranceDo not shut yourself inside “I already know”: innocence is the highest form of devotion.23God is not to be feared, but lovedFear is the opposite of love; the fear of God was learned, and your heart is the temple.24Loving the unlovableLoving what is hard for us to love, beginning with ourselves.25Everything is graceAwakening cannot be forced: it comes by grace, and devotion is the bridge.

BRANCH VII

Awakening and the life after

When the Being awakens in a human life

Once you awaken, that is when your spiritual life begins. And awakening is not reserved for yogis or monks: it is possible for every person who sincerely wishes to walk the path of the soul.

26The dark night of the soulPain is not a divine punishment: it is an alarm that teaches us compassion.27It can't be this simpleA very simple question, an innocent laugh and a volcano in the heart.28After awakening: what now?After awakening, spiritual life begins: more human, accepting all that you are.29I Am in formFrom the witness of everything to “I Am in form”: allow yourself to be human.30The saint with children, a job and debtsThe true saint lives around God in ordinary life: in the street, at the bank, at the market.31Preparing to diePreparing to die is being in clarity, living in the present.32Awakening is not a privilegeAwakening does not come to the chosen: it comes to those who are ready.
