THE QUESTION

This work did not begin as a book. It began as a life, and a question that would not let go: who is the one who looks?

The Flame Codex is the language that emerged when that question was answered, and when the answer began to descend into the body. It is not a system invented at a desk. It came after the experience, to name and accompany what was already happening.
