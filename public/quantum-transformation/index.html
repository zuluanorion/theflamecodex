Quantum Transformation

The heart of my work

This process is not only for healing. It is to bring about an awakening in your Being.

I am the originator of Quantum Transformation. It is the system that grew out of my own path: years of meditation, years in India and Thailand, years of sitting with people in healing rooms and seeing the same thing again and again. What feels like reality is often a cage, and beneath the cage something alive is waiting.

Quantum Transformation is the main work I do. Ayurveda, bioresonance, biofeedback and the evaluations all support it. Their purpose is to help the soul purify itself, unblock its energy and reconnect with the Spirit.

More than a therapy

Quantum Transformation is more than a therapeutic process for a physical, emotional or psychological ailment. It is a metamorphosis and a liberation. It is spiritual work of integration and alignment, and it opens possibilities where there were limits. You do not only resolve one issue. You awaken, and you begin a living relationship with your own Being.

It is saying to the soul: I don't need to suffer in order to learn.

The ocean of Consciousness

I like to describe us as an ocean. On the surface the waves of daily life play. Below it lies the subconscious, where what we could not process collects like a film of oil that blocks the light. Deeper still is the unconscious, where we keep what we have repressed. It stays there, quietly hurting us. And beyond all of it lies the supraconscious, the luminous level where time and space are transcended.

Behind every level is the Observer. That is your true essence, and the spiritual power that can transform your life lives there. Because the subconscious does not know linear time, we can go into the past and heal in the present.

THE PROCESS

How transformation happens

  1. Going within. Through meditation, intuition and visualization, I take you into a deeper, purer state of relaxation, beyond the thinking mind.
  2. The chakras as bridges. Each chakra is a portal to a level of Consciousness inside you. Through them we reach the place where the block was born.
  3. Recognizing what weighs. We identify the negative archetype: the emotion, the trauma or the dilemma that keeps repeating in your life.
  4. Calling in the missing light. We recognize the positive archetype, the energy your soul needs: safety where there was fear, trust where there was a wound. The angels and the luminous forces accompany this step.
  5. Synthesis. The two energies meet and the positive dissolves the negative. The tension dissipates, and a space of release, peace and fullness opens.
  6. Integration. Over the following days and weeks what was released settles into your life, until it becomes the new way you live.

IN THE SESSION

A field of presence

In a session I am not trying to fix you, and you are not trying to force your own healing. Together we hold a field of presence. In that surrender something larger than both of us acts: Consciousness itself.

The more the mind intervenes, the fewer the results. So we let go. The catharsis often comes on its own, sometimes in the session and sometimes later in dreams, memories and intense experiences that you keep integrating in the days that follow.

I have trained facilitators in this work, and they experience the same thing. When the field is held, the soul knows what to do.
